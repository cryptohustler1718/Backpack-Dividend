04 · PLAIN ENGLISH

Questions a first-time buyer asks

Does every stock pay a dividend?

No. Dividends are declared by the company, not Backpack. Some companies pay regularly; others pay nothing.

Does a tokenized dividend arrive as USDC?

For Backpack-issued tokenized securities, current Backpack materials describe the payout as automatic reinvestment into additional tokenized shares. Traditional holdings follow brokerage cash-dividend treatment. Verify the asset’s live terms.

Can I use every stock as collateral?

No. Collateral eligibility, haircuts, leverage, and regional availability are asset-specific. Confirm the live Backpack terms before borrowing.

Is a stock perpetual the same as owning shares?

No. A perpetual is a derivative contract for price exposure. It should not be described as ownership or as dividend entitlement.
